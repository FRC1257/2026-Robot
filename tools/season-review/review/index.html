How to read this

The cursor follows video time. Gray bands show disabled windows; shaded ends have no recorded telemetry. Click a chart to seek. Voltage and actuator activity describe robot behavior; they do not count scored fuel. Pose is the logged estimate, not ground truth. The DS clock may hold during packet loss.
